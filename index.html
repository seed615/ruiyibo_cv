Biography

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in the combined master-doctor program at the School of Integrated Circuits, Southeast University, under the supervision of Prof. Jiajun Zhang. I obtained my Bachelor of Engineering degree from the School of Electronic Science and Engineering, Southeast University.

My research interest lies in developing high-performance heterogeneous chips to enable efficient edge deployment of large language models (LLMs).
